Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile game Bistro Rush (the “App”) handles information. The App is developed by Agara Studio and uses the application identifier com.agara.bistrorush.

Information we collect

The App does not require an account or sign-in and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

We do not operate a personal-data server or an independent gameplay-analytics service. In advertising-enabled Android builds, Google AdMob may automatically process device and advertising information as described below.

Information stored on your device

To save your progress, the App stores game data locally on your device, including:

  • the stars earned for each day,
  • your best score for each day,
  • completed and unlocked days, determined from that progress.
  • a local frequency counter and timestamp used to limit how often advertisements appear.

We do not receive this game progress or upload it to our servers, and we do not send your scores or stars to AdMob. The App does not provide its own cloud account or cloud synchronization. Advertising-related technical information is processed separately by Google.

You can remove local progress by deleting the App and its associated data, or by clearing app data where your operating system provides that option. Your device’s backup services may retain or restore app data according to your backup settings.

Advertising

Advertising-enabled Android builds use Google AdMob (Google LLC) to display full-screen interstitial ads at natural breaks, such as leaving a completed day’s result screen. These ads are not shown during the guided tutorial or while serving customers. Other builds without advertising configuration, including the current iOS build, do not request AdMob ads.

To deliver and measure advertisements and prevent fraud, Google may automatically collect information such as:

  • device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID when available,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system, language, and application identifier,
  • advertising interactions, such as impressions and taps, and diagnostic information.

This information is processed by Google. Learn more in How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where required, the Google consent flow asks for your choices before ads may load. Available choices can be reviewed through Privacy options on the game’s main menu; availability depends on your region and Google’s consent configuration.

You can manage your advertising ID and advertising preferences in your Android device settings, usually under Settings › Privacy › Ads or Settings › Google › Ads.

Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information or share your local scores, stars, or game progress with third-party services. Google receives advertising-related technical information in advertising-enabled builds, as described above, and handles it under its privacy policies.

Children

We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information through the game. If a parent or guardian believes that their child has provided personal information to us or has a question about information processed by an advertising service, they may contact us using the email address below.

Security

Game progress is stored in the App’s local storage and is subject to the security of your device. We do not operate a server that stores this progress. Information processed by Google is subject to Google’s security practices.

This policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, such as your IP address, when you visit this website. This is separate from the game’s local progress data. You can read the GitHub General Privacy Statement for more information.

We do not add analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies to this policy page.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at agaratechid@gmail.com.

If you contact us by email, we use the information you choose to provide to respond to your message and handle your request.
